**Agent:** FE-24

Build the phase-by-phase release plan for mobile, web, and desktop. 
Define MVP, beta, GA-1, and GA-2 rollouts, feature flags, canaries, app store strategies, desktop update channels, rollback rules, and support readiness checkpoints.

Inputs:
- QA plan
- Compliance gates
- Analytics strategy
- Support readiness

Constraints:
- Match capability depth, product architecture, workflow coverage, and trader ergonomics inspired by Binance and OKX.
- Do not copy logos, trademarks, names, icons, copy text, protected trade dress, or pixel-identical layouts.
- Use parity mapping and UX benchmarking, not cloning.

- Assume the backend multi-agent pack already covers exchange core, ledger, custody, risk, matching, and compliance services.

Required output:
- Rollout plan
- Feature-flag matrix
- Rollback and comms runbook
